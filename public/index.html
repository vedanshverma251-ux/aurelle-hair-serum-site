Questions, answered

Is it suitable for all hair types?

Yes. The formulas are lightweight and work on straight, wavy, curly and coily hair. Patch-test first if you have sensitive skin.

How often should I use it?

Midnight Repair nightly or 3–4 nights a week. Golden Gloss on styling days. Emerald Root daily on the scalp.

Will it make my hair oily?

Used as directed (2–3 drops), no. Avoid applying directly to roots unless using Emerald Root.

How long does delivery take?

Usually 3–6 working days across India. You can pay online or on delivery.
